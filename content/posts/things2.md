Oh, my.  It's been more than a month since I posted my last journal.

I've been pretty overwhelmed by many things, and then, I got pretty sick for a few weeks.  This made me feel aging is real, not a joke.  It's depressing.  I didn't feel like talking to anyone, but I picked up the phone when one of my friends called me.

Then, one word she said caught my ear, FEAR.

She shared with me how strongly she's been feeling the need to grow something within herself that can overcome her fear.

I could immediately relate to it...

That heaviness I've been carrying has been weighing on me in my daily life, stemming from so many little things.  I can sense it.  Some of my worries are personal, while others are related to larger social issues, such as inflation, conflicts, and political news, but they all seem to connect back to my own personal concerns.  When can I retire?  How long will I keep going like this?

Often, my thoughts and feelings are suppressed by my own fear and cannot be voiced.  I could only see the cloud of uncertainty everywhere.

While listening to her, I kind of came to the conclusion that much of the heaviness I've been experiencing was caused by my own fear, to put it rather bluntly.

I might even say that my fear is actually a feeling of helplessness.

If I can awaken the hope that might be buried somewhere within me, despite the negative circumstances, I think I can rise above the heavy clouds.

But somehow, I'd forgotten that.

Actually, about a month ago, I decided to take a two-week vacation.  At that time, I think I had gone beyond my mental and physical limits and needed a break.  I was having this hard time deciding whether to go to my hometown or just stay home and take care of things that I've been wanting to take care.  My two weeks of vacation was initially approved, then, later my boss asked me if I could spear the last day for my co-worker.  She's more senior than I am and I remember that was her special day, so I said that's fine.  After all, I wasn't really sure if I really wanted to travel.  The ticket is quite expensive anyway.  I was hesitant to travel in economy either.  I kept looking at the ticket price every day, but I couldn't make my decision somehow.  Then, I got sick.  Well, should I say I'm superstitious, or a believer in fate?  I took it as a sign that I shouldn't travel this time.

Now, I've decided to have some quiet time for myself and sort things out.  I think I want to set some goals for this time off.  I don't want these two weeks to just slip through my fingers.  Will I ever be able to shake off this heaviness?
