Honestly, I was seriously thinking about going back to my hometown.  But, at the same time, I knew that wherever you go, there is no paradise.  If you get away from one problem, before you can even sit down on your favorite couch and sip your favorite drink, something else will come up in front of you.

That's life.

The urge to run away from the heaviness has been overpowering me, yet I knew there was no escape.  I mean, nothing wrong with running away.  Sometimes it's necessary to avoid predictable negative consequences.  What I'm talking about here seems to be something different...

Despite the helplessness, after all, I don't want to be a loser.  The kind of loser who avoids the fair share of what it means to be human.  Although I can't explain what it is, I sense that each one of us owes something to our fellow human beings through the way we live.  Maybe it's about living fully as yourself?  I feel even more depressed when I think about the misery I might face after running away.

I have to choose.

Not running away impulsively.

A conscious decision.

You know.

The social climate changed drastically overnight.  Thank goodness.  I have a feeling that the road ahead of us won't be an easy one.  Yet, I feel as if my tense muscles are slowly relaxing in the spring sunshine.

That's more than enough for now.
